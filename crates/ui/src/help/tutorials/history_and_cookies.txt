See the requests that you sent, send one again, and manage the cookies.

── History ──
  1.  Press {key:history}. The sidebar shows the history: the requests that you sent, in groups by day.
  2.  Select an entry with Up and Down. The status of each entry shows in its color.
  3.  Press Enter to open a copy of the entry in a new tab. The response pane shows the response that Sendito kept, with "history: <time>" in the border.
  4.  Press Ctrl+Enter or F5 to send the entry again. Sendito opens a copy and sends it.
  5.  Press s to save the entry in the tree.
  6.  Press {key:history} to go back to the tree.
The history keeps the secrets masked. The settings Record history, History
entries kept and History response body set what the history keeps. The
command :clearhistory deletes all the entries.

── Cookies ──
  •  The cookie jar keeps the cookies of the responses and sends them again, as a browser does. Each environment has its own jar.
  •  The Cookie jar field of the Options tab turns it on or off for one request. The setting Cookie jar is the default.
  •  Type :cookies to see the cookies of the active environment. The values are masked.
  •  In the list, press Enter on a cookie to delete it. The last row clears all the cookies.
  •  The commands :cookies clear and :cookies delete <number> do the same from the command palette.
